## What this product is
Team Rota is a shift roster for one small team: a planner publishes the week, members see who holds what, and a member hands a shift to a willing counterpart through a swap the planner approves — one place where the week, the swaps and their reasons are visible to everyone concerned.
**North Star (every UX and product decision is judged against it):** The roster tells each person the truth about their week — who holds every shift, what is still pending, and why a request was refused — plainly, even when the truth is unwelcome.
**What it must never become:** an optimiser that assigns people for the planner; a chat; a silent system that changes state without telling the people affected; a multi-team product (this version).
