1. design/domains/notifications/spec.md line 13 [BLOCKING] — Problem: The notifications domain's dependency direction is contradictory and the Notifier it depends on is undefined. Stories US-notifications-001 (line 21), -002, -003 and -004 (line 21 each) say notifications reads Week through scheduling's interface and SwapRequest/Decision through swaps' interface, yet no IR row exists in either direction and this spec lists only identity (IR-04). Strike the 'read through swaps'/'scheduling' claims from US-notifications-001..004 and 'member names for notification text' from IR-09.
